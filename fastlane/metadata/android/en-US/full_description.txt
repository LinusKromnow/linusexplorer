LinusExplorer is a free and open-source file manager. No ads, no tracking, no paid features.

<b>Files on your device</b>
<ul>
<li>Browse internal storage, SD cards and the whole file system</li>
<li>Copy, move, rename, delete and share files and folders; long copies keep running in the background with a progress notification</li>
<li>Recycle bin: deleted files can be restored for 30 days</li>
<li>List and grid views with image, video and APK thumbnails</li>
<li>Recursive search with wildcards</li>
<li>Bookmarks and recently visited folders</li>
<li>Tabs, and two panes side by side on tablets and foldables</li>
<li>Folder sizes and checksums (MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256) in the properties dialog</li>
<li>Built-in text editor with find and replace, undo and line numbers</li>
<li>Optional root access on rooted devices</li>
</ul>

<b>Network storage</b>
<ul>
<li>SFTP, FTP and FTPS, SMB 2/3 (Windows shares, Samba, NAS), WebDAV (Nextcloud, ownCloud) and S3-compatible storage (AWS, Backblaze B2, Cloudflare R2, Wasabi, MinIO)</li>
<li>Saved connections are stored encrypted on the device</li>
<li>Edit a remote file in any app and upload the changes back</li>
</ul>

<b>Archives and privacy</b>
<ul>
<li>Open, extract and create ZIP, 7z and tar archives, including AES-256 password-protected ZIPs</li>
<li>Encrypt single files with the open age format</li>
<li>Private vault: an encrypted folder, unlocked with a password or fingerprint, with backup and restore</li>
<li>App lock with PIN or fingerprint</li>
</ul>

The app only goes online for servers you add yourself. Material You design with light and dark themes.
