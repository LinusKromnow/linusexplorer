LinusExplorer is a free and open-source file manager. No ads, no tracking, no paid features.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Browse internal storage, SD cards and the root file system</li>
<li>Copy, move, rename, delete and share files and folders</li>
<li>Multi-select, natural sorting, hidden files toggle</li>
<li>Material You design with light and dark themes</li>
</ul>

<b>Coming soon</b>
<ul>
<li>SFTP, FTP/FTPS, SMB and WebDAV</li>
<li>Cloud storage: Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Nextcloud, S3 and more</li>
<li>Archives, image thumbnails, search, bookmarks and tabs</li>
</ul>
